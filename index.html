Oğuz Yurtoğlu

PhD Candidate in Economics, Koç University

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Economics at Koç University. My research is in time series econometrics and regional economics, with a focus on housing markets and on how shocks propagate across them through networks of migration, distance, and land-use regulation.

My dissertation studies housing market networks in the United States using the Diebold–Yılmaz connectedness framework. My advisor is Prof. Kamil Yılmaz.

From October 2024 to January 2026 I was a research assistant at BETAM, the Bahçeşehir University Center for Economic and Social Research, where I co-authored the quarterly growth assessments and a research note on deep poverty, and worked on a survey-based report on the Istanbul labor market for the Istanbul Metropolitan Municipality.
